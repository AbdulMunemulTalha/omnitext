Questions

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. Customers keep messaging your Page, Instagram or WhatsApp as they do today. Your replies from Quicky arrive in the same chat.

Can I still use the Facebook Page app on my phone?

Yes. Replies you send from Meta Business Suite also show up in Quicky, so the conversation history stays complete.

What happens if a moderator is off duty?

Their customers stay with them, and you can move any customer to someone else. New customers go only to moderators who are on duty; if nobody is, they wait in "Unassigned" until someone starts.

Is my data safe?

Connections are encrypted, Facebook and WhatsApp access is stored encrypted, and moderators only see their own customers. Read the privacy policy.
